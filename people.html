Apil KC

Chief Executive Officer and Managing Director

Apil leads the firm’s strategy and its work on disaster recovery, resilient infrastructure and urban planning. He is a PhD candidate in Urban and Regional Planning at the University of Michigan’s Taubman College, where he studies how recovery is governed after disasters in Nepal and Sri Lanka.

Alongside the firm, he advises Nepal’s Ministry of Health and Population on climate-resilient health facilities as a Climate Resilient Infrastructure Expert under the SAMARTHA programme. His research has appeared in the International Journal of Disaster Risk Reduction.

The advisory and research roles above are Apil’s individual experience, separate from the firm’s own assignments.
