Bethauli-Kaparkatti Lake and Park, Butwal

A detailed project report to open the lake to the public while protecting its water source and ecology.

AssignmentDetailed project report
LocationButwal, Rupandehi
PeriodDecember 2020
RoleSub-consultant, technical experts
ValueNPR 3.5 million (US$ 19,750)

Bethauli-Kaparkatti Lake is one of Butwal’s key natural assets. The brief was to open it to the public while protecting its water source and ecology.

We kept building to a minimum. The design protects the lake’s water source, softens the edges with planting, and adds pedestrian paths, a footbridge and low-level lighting. Every route is accessible.

The result is a public space for boating, fishing, cycling, walking and community events.

What we did

  • Landscape planning and design
  • Urban design and accessibility planning
  • Recreational infrastructure design
  • Footbridge design coordination
  • Lighting design
  • Environmental conservation measures and assessment
  • Cost estimate and construction documents

Experts mobilised

  • Urban Planner
  • Senior Architect
  • Bio-engineering Expert
  • Geotechnical Engineer
  • Hydrologist
  • Site Engineer
