Tulsi Hospital, Dang: detailed engineering design

Design and supervision for our first 100-bed hospital.

AssignmentDetailed engineering design and supervision
LocationDang
Period2019 to 2021
RoleDesign and supervision consultant
ValueNPR 2.5 million (US$ 16,500)

Tulsi Hospital was our first 100-bed health facility, and the approach we developed here still guides our health infrastructure work.

Architecture and engineering were designed together: a seismic-resistant structural system, barrier-free layouts that follow how patients and staff move, and coordinated services. We prepared the environmental assessment and a complete tender package, then supervised construction.

What we did

  • Site survey and soil investigation
  • Architectural, structural, electrical and sanitary design
  • Environmental assessment
  • Cost estimate and BOQ
  • Technical specifications and tender documents
  • Construction supervision

Experts mobilised

  • Team Leader and Senior Architect
  • Architect
  • Structural Engineer
  • Electrical Engineer
  • Sanitary Engineer
  • Geotechnical Engineer
  • Environmental Specialist
  • Survey Engineer
  • Quantity Surveyor
  • CAD Draftsperson
