About

our story.

We began as an architecture studio and moved, project by project, into planning, disaster risk and research. We still work close to the site and to the people who use it.

Our story

  1. 2014

    A design studio in Dang

    A small architecture practice in western Nepal, making buildings that worked well and fitted their place.

  2. 2015

    Incorporated

    Registered on 27 May 2015 as Ingenious Design Solution Pvt. Ltd.

  3. 2019 to 2022

    Hospitals, parks and a lake

    Two 100-bed hospitals in Dang, and detailed project reports for a public park in Dang and a lake and park in Butwal.

  4. 2021

    Landslide and flood mitigation

    Landslide and flood mitigation in Birendranagar and Dullu, combining civil works with bio-engineering.

  5. 2023

    A new name and a Kathmandu office

    We became Resilient Collaborative and moved our registered office to Kathmandu. The same year we fielded the survey team for a national baseline on building energy use.

  6. Today

    Today

    A multi-disciplinary practice working for governments, municipalities and development partners, with research feeding into planning and engineering.

Our vision

Places that work for the people who live in them, now and for generations to come.

We plan, design and engineer public places and infrastructure with governments, communities and development partners across Nepal and South Asia. Our plans rest on field evidence and local knowledge. We design them to withstand disasters and a changing climate, and we stay involved until they are built.

Principles

Plans should still work after the next disaster. We design with floods and earthquakes in mind, and with the budgets that will be available to maintain what is built.

We work in mixed teams and with local partners. Engineers hear from social specialists, and communities see drawings before they are final.

We consult before we design. Ideas are tested with the people who will use the result.

We are open about uncertainty. Our figures can be traced to their sources, and our advice is independent.

We carry lessons forward. What we learn on one assignment informs the next.
