Work · Disaster risk

Landslide and flood mitigation in Birendranagar and Dullu

Civil works and bio-engineering to stabilise slopes and riverbanks affected by monsoon landslides and flooding.

ClientTayar Nepal
ProgrammeImproved Disaster Risk Management Project
LocationBirendranagar, Surkhet and Dullu, Dailekh
PeriodJune to December 2021
RoleSub-consultant, technical experts
ValueNPR 19.07 million (US$ 144,388)

Birendranagar and Dullu live with recurring landslides and river flooding. Under the Improved Disaster Risk Management Project, Tayar Nepal engaged us to provide the technical experts for mitigation works in both municipalities.

Our design paired civil engineering with bio-engineering. Gabion walls, embankments and drainage hold the slopes and riverbanks in place. Crib walls, brush layering, fascines, palisades, bamboo fencing and planting help the slopes stabilise naturally in the seasons that follow.

Alongside the works we ran training for communities and municipal staff and supported local enterprise, so that local people can maintain the works.

What we did

  • Site survey and assessment
  • Flood and landslide mitigation design support
  • Slope stabilisation and riverbank protection
  • Gabion, stone masonry and drainage structures
  • Bio-engineering: crib walls, brush layering, fascines, palisades, bamboo fencing and planting
  • Construction supervision, quantity checks and quality control
  • Community training and enterprise development support

Experts mobilised

  • Urban Planner
  • Bio-engineering Expert
  • Geotechnical Engineer
  • Hydrologist
  • Landslide Expert
  • Enterprise Development Expert
  • Curriculum Development Expert
  • Site Engineer
