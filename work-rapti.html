Rapti Hospital, Dang: detailed engineering design

Design and supervision for a 100-bed hospital in a seismically active region.

AssignmentDetailed engineering design and supervision
LocationDang
Period2020 to 2022
RoleDesign and supervision consultant
ValueNPR 3.5 million (US$ 23,000)

A hospital in an earthquake-prone region needs to remain operational after a disaster.

We designed architecture and engineering together from the start: a seismic-resistant structure, barrier-free and patient-centred planning, and mechanical, electrical and plumbing services coordinated with both. An environmental assessment and a full tender package followed, and we stayed on to supervise construction.

What we did

  • Site survey and soil investigation
  • Architectural, structural, electrical and sanitary design
  • Environmental assessment
  • Cost estimate and BOQ
  • Technical specifications and tender documents
  • Construction supervision

Experts mobilised

  • Team Leader and Senior Architect
  • Architect
  • Structural Engineer
  • Electrical Engineer
  • Sanitary Engineer
  • Geotechnical Engineer
  • Environmental Specialist
  • Survey Engineer
  • Quantity Surveyor
  • CAD Draftsperson
